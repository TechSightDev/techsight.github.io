What I look for

Small SaaS deals go wrong in predictable ways

  • The ARR isn't really ARR. It's last month × 12, or it includes services and pass‑through fees.
  • One person holds the whole system in their head. Usually the founder, and they leave after the transition period.
  • The "AI" is a thin wrapper, or the product is a seat‑based workflow tool that AI is about to commoditize.
  • The hosting, security, and backup story doesn't hold up. It's fine until the first outage after you've signed the personal guarantee.
