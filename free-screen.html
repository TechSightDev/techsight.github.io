Free Red‑Flag Screen

Send me the deal documents before you sign the LOI. Within 3 business days, you get a 2‑page memo telling you whether the tech supports the deal, what could break it, and what full diligence should dig into.

Book a 30‑minute intake Or email the CIM
Who it's for

Buyers of small B2B SaaS, before the LOI

Self‑funded searchers, search funds, and holdcos or micro‑PE funds looking at $250K–$5M ARR B2B SaaS, whether it came through Acquire.com, Quiet Light, FE International, Rejigg, or a broker.

What you get

A 2‑page memo within 3 business days

  • Executive read: proceed, proceed with focus, or serious concerns
  • Red flags, prioritized and in plain language
  • What full diligence should focus on for this target
  • What I couldn't assess from the documents provided

I look at four areas: whether the architecture supports your growth thesis, team and key‑person risk, hosting and disaster‑recovery posture, and whether the tech and AI claims hold up.

What to send
  • The CIM, teaser, or listing (PDF or link)
  • Any tech appendix or architecture overview the seller has shared
  • An engineering org snapshot: who builds and runs it, and anyone the business depends on
  • A paragraph on your thesis: why this deal, and what worries you about the tech

No code or system access needed. The 3‑day clock starts once I have these. If something's missing, I'll tell you within 1 business day.

What it isn't

The screen is a fast read from the documents, not a substitute for diligence. It doesn't include a code review, interviews with the seller's team, a security audit, a valuation opinion, or costed remediation.

If the deal moves forward, those come with technical diligence, quoted at a fixed fee.

Conflicts

I buy small SaaS companies too. I never screen a deal I'm pursuing, and I never pursue a deal I've screened for you.
