Free Red-Flag Screen

First screen free per firm. Document-based only. 2–4 hours of review time (hard cap 4) with an optional ≤30‑minute clarifier call inside that cap. You get a 2‑page memo in 3 business days.

Who It's For

Buyers of Small B2B SaaS, Pre‑LOI

Self‑funded searchers, traditional search funds, and small holdcos / micro‑PE buying $250K–$5M ARR B2B SaaS, pre‑LOI. Also applicable to tech‑enabled services evaluations. Many deals come from marketplaces and brokers (Acquire.com, Quiet Light, FE International, Rejigg, etc.).

What To Send
  • CIM / broker teaser / public listing (PDF or link)
  • Any technical appendix or architecture overview available
  • Engineering org snapshot (roles, headcount, key‑person notes if known)
  • Your short thesis (why this deal; top risks you see)

Document‑based only. No repository access, interviews, or code‑level review at this stage.

What You Get

A 2‑Page Memo in 3 Business Days

  • Executive read: proceed / proceed‑with‑focus / serious concerns
  • Prioritized red flags in plain language
  • What full diligence should focus on for this target
  • What we could not review based on the documents provided

Includes an optional ≤30‑minute clarifier discussion inside the same time cap.

What’s Not Included
  • No repository or code‑level deep‑dive
  • No interviews with the seller’s team
  • No costed risk register or integration plan
  • No IC deck

Those are part of a paid Small‑SaaS Technical Diligence engagement on step 2 of our ladder.

Conflicts & Disclosure

We also buy small SaaS companies. To avoid conflicts: we never screen a deal we’re pursuing, and we never pursue a deal we screened for you. This rule is published here and on our About page.
