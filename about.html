About TechSight

How We're Different

Screens from an operator, not a vendor

Most “screens” are pitch decks and interviews. Ours are built from the documents in hand and the questions small SaaS buyers actually need answered pre‑LOI. Findings are translated into deal language: severity, likelihood, and where to focus full diligence — not buzzwords.

When it’s time to go deeper, we run fixed‑fee, time‑boxed diligence sized for small deals, then stay post‑close to execute when helpful.

Who This Is For

Buyers of small B2B SaaS

Self‑funded searchers, search funds, and small holdcos / micro‑PE buying $250K–$5M ARR B2B SaaS, pre‑LOI. Messaging may lean on PropTech, FinTech/accounting, and HealthTech — without hard gates.

  • Pre‑LOI, document‑based Free Red‑Flag Screen (first screen free per firm)
  • Fixed‑fee, code‑level diligence sized for small deals
  • After close: execution and fractional CTO when helpful

We speak both sides: ARR and DSCR on one side; architecture, technical debt, and key‑person risk on the other.

Conflicts

We buy small SaaS too

To avoid conflicts, we follow a simple rule: we never screen a deal we’re pursuing, and we never pursue a deal we screened for you.

The rule applies to the free screen and to paid diligence.
