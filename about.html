I screen small SaaS deals. I buy them too.

TechSight is me, {{ site.founder_name }}. I'm a CTO who reviews software acquisitions for buyers and runs my own search for B2B SaaS to buy.

Why that matters

I'm on the same side of the table as you

I'm looking for small B2B SaaS companies myself, with a written thesis and a 100‑point rubric. I read CIMs, write up assessments, and make offers. When I screen a deal for you, I ask the questions I'd ask before putting my own name on a personal guarantee.

That means I care about the things that hurt small buyers: ARR quality, customer concentration, whether one person holds the whole system in their head, whether the product survives AI, and what it'll cost to run once the seller is gone.

I've spent my career on the technical side of B2B software, with the most depth in CRM/ERP, PropTech, FinTech and accounting, HealthTech, and data integration. So I can tell you what's in the code, not just what's in the deck.

One rule, published

The rule covers free screens and paid diligence. If a deal you send overlaps with one I'm looking at, I'll tell you and decline the screen.
