How It Works

Start with a free screen. Pay for diligence only if the deal earns it. Bring me in after close if you want help running the tech.

Step 1 · Before the LOI · Free

Red‑flag screen

Send me the CIM or listing and whatever tech overview the seller has shared. Within 3 business days, I send back a 2‑page memo with an executive read, prioritized red flags, what full diligence should focus on, and what I couldn't assess.

Documents only, no code access needed. One free screen per buyer.

Step 2 · LOI to close · Fixed fee

Technical diligence

A code‑level review sized for $250K–$5M ARR targets. It covers the codebase, architecture and infrastructure, security posture, and key‑person risk.

You get a costed risk register: each finding with its severity and an estimated cost to fix. You can use it in price, escrow, and transition negotiations, and it becomes your post‑close plan.

Fixed fee, quoted after the screen once I know the scope, and scheduled around your exclusivity window.

Step 3 · After close · Optional

Refactor and run

Most small‑SaaS buyers don't have a technical team when they take over. I turn the risk register into a first‑100‑days plan, covering stability, hosting costs, monitoring, and the release process, and I can stay on as fractional CTO while you hire or grow.
